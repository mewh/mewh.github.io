04 — Selected Work

Work that holds up
in the real world.

We do not hide the complexity of real work. We connect information, make responsibility visible and build systems people can rely on. These are selected projects delivered by members of our team.

01

Power Generation & Engineering

Mitsubishi Power Africa (Pty) Ltd ↗

Docwize supports Mitsubishi Power Africa across two large coal-fired power stations in South Africa. We consolidated project documentation and data that had been fragmented across multiple systems into a single controlled environment, and we manage the hand-over documentation process and tracking end to end. Our team also built large-scale power-generation sensor analytics, processing billions of datapoints into dashboards.

02

EPCM & Engineering Document Control

METC Engineering ↗

METC Engineering is a South African–headquartered EPCM firm that designs and builds metallurgical processing plants and mining infrastructure on projects across Africa. Docwize provides engineering document control across their concurrent projects, covering drawings, specifications, transmittals, revisions, reviews and deadlines. Workflows are used extensively — every document carries a review step, a responsible party and a due date, with tens of thousands of workflow actions tracked.

03

Public Programme & Compliance

Working on Fire ↗

Working on Fire is a South African government-backed wildfire management programme that recruits, trains and deploys young men and women as firefighters from community bases across the country, and has sent crews on international deployments including to Canada. Docwize maintains the programme's compliance registers — over 138,000 beneficiary records with employment, payroll and training data linked at individual level, and 50,000 supporting documents connected to the records they support. Monthly compliance reporting that previously required manual cross-system reconciliation now runs in under 10 minutes, with unresolved evidence gaps flagged automatically.

04

Construction & Information Governance

Stefanutti Stocks ↗

Stefanutti Stocks is a JSE-listed multidisciplinary construction group, one of South Africa's largest, delivering infrastructure projects of any scale across South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa, and certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and ISO 27001. Docwize holds their company and project documentation, with their in-house systems integrated directly through the Docwize API. Workflows drive the distribution of documents and the checking of incoming supplier invoices, each validated against vendor, purchase order and goods-received before approval. Docwize also serves as their long-term document archive alongside existing collaboration tools — every document retains its revision, recipient and issue date, so when a claim surfaces after project handover the record can still be evidenced.

05

Mining & Contract Management

African Rainbow Minerals (ARM) ↗

African Rainbow Minerals is a JSE-listed diversified South African mining company producing iron ore, manganese, chrome, platinum group metals, nickel and coal, with manganese alloy production in Malaysia and a strategic gold investment through Harmony Gold. ARM uses Docwize as their contract management and record keeping system, deployed on-premise on their own infrastructure.
